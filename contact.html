Frequently Asked Questions

How do I search for properties?

Visit our Listings page and use the filter options to search by location, price range, and property type. You can also use the search bar on the homepage.

How do I contact a landlord?

Click on any property to view details, then use the contact form on that page to send a message directly to the landlord. They'll respond within 24-48 hours.

Are all properties verified?

Yes, all properties on our platform are verified and inspected by our team to ensure they meet our standards for safety and quality.

What if I need to schedule a viewing?

Contact the landlord directly through our platform. They can arrange a convenient time for you to visit the property.

Is there a registration fee?

No, registration and browsing listings are completely free. We only charge landlords a listing fee, not the students.

How can I report a problem?

Use this contact form with subject "Technical Issue" or email us directly at support@moicampushomes.com with details.
